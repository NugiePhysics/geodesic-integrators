# Pitfalls

Twenty ways a geodesic integration can go wrong without failing loudly, collected before the project started and checked against what actually happened. The code and the other documents refer to them by number ("pitfall 18"). The last column says where each one showed up here; the [development log](devlog.md) has the details.

| # | Pitfall | Symptom | Mitigation | Seen here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinate singularity at $`r = 2M`$ ($`g_{rr} \to \infty`$; in (b), $`p_r = \dot r / f`$ diverges) | Adaptive steps shrink without end, NaN, evaluation budget exhausted | Stop at $`r = 2M(1 + \delta)`$ by root finding; report the status "captured"; to cross the horizon use Kerr–Schild coordinates | Every capture stops cleanly at the stopping surface; the metric kernels use $`r - 2M`$ to stay accurate there |
| 2 | Polar singularity $`\theta = 0, \pi`$ ($`1/\sin^2\theta`$) | $`\dot p_\theta`$ blows up on highly inclined orbits | Keep inclinations at or below 85°, or rotate coordinates; Cartesian Kerr–Schild in the future | At 85° every method is under-resolved at 1000 steps per period, (b) worse than (a); Tao4 fails (F18) |
| 3 | $`\cos(\pi/2) \approx 6\times10^{-17} \neq 0`$ | An equatorial orbit leaves the plane by a tiny amount | Expected (a tilt of about $`10^{-16}`$); document it, do not treat it as a bug | It is what eventually tips the equatorial photon-sphere orbit after about 900 orbits |
| 4 | Choice of the affine parameter | Null results not comparable between cases; tolerances meaningless | Null: normalize $`E = 1`$. Timelike: $`\lambda = \tau`$. Compare at events or at equal $`t`$, not at equal $`\lambda`$ across formulations | Built into the test cases |
| 5 | Cyclic $`t`$, $`\phi`$ grow without bound | `rtol` becomes loose; $`\phi`$ is resolved only to its ulp ($`7\times10^{-12}`$ at $`\phi = 6\times10^4`$) | Per-component `atol` or exclude $`t`$ from the norm; compensated summation | GL3's phase error after $`10^4`$ orbits is 5 ulp of $`\phi`$ (F9) |
| 6 | Initial data off the constraint | $`H`$ drifts from step zero | Solve the shell for the momentum with a cancellation-free formula; test $`\lvert C_0\rvert \le 10^{-15}`$ | Near turning points $`p_r`$ came out as $`\sqrt{\epsilon_\text{mach}}`$; bound orbits now start at the turning point |
| 7 | Events by linear interpolation | Measured order stuck at 2 | Dense output of matching order, or root finding on partial steps; ignore the spurious event at $`\lambda = 0`$ when starting at $`p_r = 0`$ | The predecessor's "analytic" precession was evaluated at a sampled periapsis; events are now located by partial steps |
| 8 | Comparing at `t_eval` | DOP853 appears to have a lower order (the interpolant is less accurate) | Compare the final state at the end of the span | Avoided by design |
| 9 | `rtol` below about $`10^{-13}`$ | Error floor, SciPy warnings | Limit sweeps; show the round-off floor | Caps DOP853's phase error over 1000 periods at $`4.8\times10^{-6}`$ (F20) |
| 10 | Implicit iteration stopped too early | A "symplectic" method drifts linearly | Iterate to stagnation; measure the effect | GL2 drifts tenfold over 1000 periods with tolerance $`10^{-6}`$ or $`10^{-8}`$ (F15) |
| 11 | Adaptive steps with a symplectic method | Bounded energy error is lost | Fixed steps; Sundman or Poincaré time transformations for variable steps | Not used here; listed as an extension |
| 12 | Long-term round-off (Brouwer's law) | $`\lvert\Delta H\rvert \propto \sqrt\lambda`$, or even linear from biased rounding | Compensated summation; $`\lambda = \lambda_0 + nh`$ | Plain summation: phase error $`5.5\times10^{-6}`$ growing like $`N^2`$ against $`5\times10^{-11}`$ compensated; it also fakes photon-sphere survival (F19) |
| 13 | Tuning $`\omega`$ in Tao's method | Copies separate, or the error grows | Sweep $`\omega`$, monitor $`\lVert q - x\rVert`$, report the sensitivity | $`\omega = 10^{-3}`$ fails on the ISCO and on inclined orbits ([ADR 0006](decisions/0006-tao-coupling.md)) |
| 14 | Unfair timing | SciPy looks slow because of Python overhead, not the algorithm | Evaluations as the primary cost; time only our own compiled code; warm-up; median of repeats; one thread | [ADR 0003](decisions/0003-nfev-as-cost-metric.md) |
| 15 | Counting evaluations | Counts not comparable between methods | Define one evaluation, including implicit iterations and Tao's gradients; SciPy also counts the initial step selection | SciPy's count with events includes 3 extra evaluations per event, ours 20–50 (partial steps) |
| 16 | JAX defaults to float32 | Errors stall at $`10^{-7}`$ | Enable `jax_enable_x64` first | JAX not used yet |
| 17 | Elliptic-function conventions ($`k`$ against $`m = k^2`$ differ between SciPy, mpmath and books) | A silently wrong reference | Test limits; two independent reference paths | Caught a mistranscribed Darwin formula at once (Phase 4) |
| 18 | Deflection compared with the asymptotic angle when the integration stops at $`r_\text{far}`$ | A systematic error $`O(b/r_\text{far}^2)`$ hides the integrator error | Compare $`\Delta\phi(r_\text{far})`$ with the exact integral to the same $`r_\text{far}`$ | Showed up once more in the first version of F2 |
| 19 | Ill-conditioning near $`b_c`$ and the photon sphere ($`d\alpha/db \to \infty`$) | Large errors mistaken for a weakness of the method | Report the condition number; separate method error from physical amplification | Winding errors follow $`1/(b - b_c)`$ exactly (F12) |
| 20 | Fixed $`\lambda`$ steps on very eccentric orbits | Steps wasted at apoapsis | A legitimate finding for work-precision; explain it; time transformations as an extension | Visible in the step-size histories (F16) |
